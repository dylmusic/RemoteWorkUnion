Best Remote Work Jobs for Sales Reps and Account Executives

Sales experience is one of the strongest foundations for remote work. This guide breaks down the best paths — from traditional remote sales to AI training roles where your judgment gets paid.

Sales is one of the strongest backgrounds for remote work because good salespeople already understand communication, persuasion, research, follow-up, buyer psychology, and business judgment. Those skills transfer well into traditional work from home sales roles, customer-facing remote jobs, sales operations, account management, business development, and newer AI training jobs where companies need humans to evaluate whether AI systems understand real business conversations.

A sales rep does not need to become a software engineer to work remotely. An account executive does not need to start over in an entry-level job. The better strategy is to identify which parts of your sales experience are most valuable outside a traditional office: handling objections, explaining complex offers clearly, qualifying leads, spotting weak messaging, comparing buyer responses, writing follow-up emails, working inside a CRM, and knowing what a serious prospect sounds like.

Why Sales Experience Works Well for Remote Jobs

Remote companies care about output. They want people who can communicate clearly, manage their own pipeline, document work, use online tools, and make decisions without constant supervision. Sales professionals often build those habits earlier than people in many other fields.

A strong sales rep is used to tracking activity, working from a quota, following up without being reminded, and adjusting messaging when something does not convert. A strong account executive is used to leading discovery calls, identifying buyer pain, reading between the lines, and turning messy conversations into clear next steps. Those are remote-friendly skills.

The best remote job search for a sales professional should include direct sales roles, customer success roles, account management, sales operations, partnerships, revenue operations, and AI model evaluation roles related to business communication.

1. Remote Account Executive Jobs

Remote account executive jobs are the most obvious path for experienced salespeople. These roles usually involve discovery calls, demos, proposals, negotiation, CRM updates, follow-up, and closing new business. Many companies hire account executives remotely because the work already happens through video calls, email, CRM software, and digital sales materials.

Good search terms include: remote account executive, SaaS account executive, B2B account executive remote, closing role remote, mid-market account executive, enterprise account executive, full-cycle sales remote, and work from home account executive.

2. Remote SDR and BDR Jobs

Sales development representative and business development representative jobs are common remote sales roles. SDRs and BDRs usually focus on lead research, outbound emails, cold calls, LinkedIn messages, qualification, and booking meetings for account executives.

A sales rep with previous cold calling or appointment-setting experience should not undersell that background. Companies value people who can handle rejection, write clean outreach, keep a CRM updated, and stay organized across dozens or hundreds of prospects.

Good search terms include: remote SDR, remote BDR, sales development representative work from home, business development representative remote, appointment setter remote, outbound sales remote, and lead generation specialist remote.

3. Remote Account Manager Jobs

Remote account manager jobs are often better for sales professionals who prefer relationships over constant prospecting. Account managers usually work with existing customers, help them get value from a product or service, renew contracts, identify upsell opportunities, and prevent churn.

This is one of the best remote work paths for sales reps who are tired of cold outreach but still like business conversations. The skills that matter most are communication, follow-through, customer empathy, product understanding, and the ability to spot expansion opportunities without sounding pushy.

4. Customer Success Manager Roles

Customer success is not the same thing as sales, but the overlap is strong. Customer success managers help customers onboard, use a product, solve problems, and continue paying for the service. In many companies, customer success also touches renewals, expansion, account health, and product feedback.

This can be a good remote job for sales reps who are consultative, patient, and good at explaining value. Good search terms include: remote customer success manager, customer success associate remote, client success specialist remote, onboarding specialist remote, and customer experience manager remote.

5. Sales Operations and Revenue Operations Jobs

Sales operations and revenue operations roles are excellent for sales professionals who understand systems. These jobs usually involve CRM hygiene, pipeline reporting, dashboards, forecasting, lead routing, sales process improvement, and data cleanup.

This path is especially useful for sales reps who are strong with Salesforce, HubSpot, spreadsheets, dashboards, or automation tools. It is also a good option for people who want remote work without spending the whole day on calls.

Good search terms include: remote sales operations specialist, revenue operations analyst remote, RevOps coordinator remote, CRM specialist remote, Salesforce administrator remote, HubSpot specialist remote, and pipeline analyst remote.

6. Remote Sales Enablement Jobs

Sales enablement roles help sales teams perform better. The work can include writing scripts, building training materials, improving pitch decks, creating objection-handling guides, documenting processes, reviewing calls, and helping new reps ramp faster.

This is a strong option for experienced sales reps and account executives who are good at teaching others. Good search terms include: remote sales enablement, sales trainer remote, sales coach remote, sales content specialist, enablement specialist remote, and revenue enablement remote.

7. Partnership and Business Development Roles

Partnership roles are a good fit for salespeople who are strong at relationship-building but do not want a standard closing role. These jobs can involve finding partner companies, building referral channels, negotiating collaborations, managing affiliates, or coordinating co-marketing opportunities.

8. AI Training Jobs for Sales Professionals

AI training is one of the most interesting remote work categories for sales reps and account executives because it can pay for judgment rather than availability. AI companies and AI platforms need people to evaluate whether AI outputs are accurate, useful, persuasive, safe, and realistic. Sales professionals can be useful when the tasks involve business communication, customer conversations, CRM context, lead qualification, objection handling, negotiation, or buyer psychology.

A sales professional might be asked to compare two AI-written sales emails, judge whether a chatbot handled a customer objection correctly, write realistic buyer personas, evaluate a sales assistant's recommendation, review a CRM-related answer, or test prompts that simulate business conversations.

This kind of work may appear under titles like AI evaluator, AI trainer, model evaluator, AI data annotator, response evaluator, business writing evaluator, sales conversation evaluator, prompt writer, or AI research contributor. Platforms that route talent into AI projects may work with companies connected to major AI ecosystems, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and Grok.

For salespeople interested in AI training: The strongest applicants are usually clear writers who can explain why one answer is better than another. Focus your profile on business communication, buyer psychology, response evaluation, and sales email review skills.

Best Remote Jobs for Sales Reps Who Hate Cold Calling

Not every sales professional wants to keep calling prospects all day. If you want remote work but do not want a phone-heavy role, focus on jobs where your sales background is useful but the day-to-day work is more writing, analysis, systems, or evaluation.

The best options usually include sales operations, revenue operations, CRM specialist roles, sales enablement, proposal writing, RFP work, AI model evaluation, sales content, customer research, onboarding, and some account management roles.

How Sales Reps Should Rewrite Their Resume for Remote Work

A remote sales resume should not only say that you are persuasive. It should prove that you can work independently, manage systems, communicate in writing, and produce measurable outcomes.

Include the sales tools you have used, such as Salesforce, HubSpot, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Outreach, Apollo, ZoomInfo, Gong, Slack, Notion, and Google Workspace. Mention remote-friendly skills like pipeline management, async communication, written follow-up, lead research, forecasting, documentation, and cross-functional collaboration.

For AI training or evaluation roles, add: business communication • buyer psychology • response evaluation • prompt writing • sales email review • quality assessment • customer conversation analysis • CRM context • model output evaluation

What to Avoid

Avoid remote jobs that ask you to pay for training, buy leads upfront, purchase a starter kit, or work entirely on commission with vague promises. Commission-only work can be legitimate in some contexts, but it should be evaluated carefully. A serious company should be clear about the product, compensation, expected activity, tools, training, and how results are tracked.

FAQs

Can sales reps find remote work without cold calling?

Yes. Sales operations, revenue operations, CRM specialist roles, sales enablement, proposal writing, RFP work, AI model evaluation, and some account management roles use sales judgment without requiring daily outbound phone calls.

What remote AI jobs can sales professionals do?

Sales professionals can apply for AI evaluator, AI trainer, model evaluator, response evaluator, business writing evaluator, sales conversation evaluator, and prompt writer roles. These use business communication judgment without requiring coding experience.

How do I rewrite my sales resume for remote work?

Emphasize remote-friendly skills: pipeline management, async communication, written follow-up, lead research, CRM tools, and cross-functional collaboration. For AI training roles, add phrases like business communication, buyer psychology, response evaluation, and sales email review.

What is the best path for a sales professional who wants less calling?

Sales operations, CRM work, sales enablement, proposal writing, customer research, and AI training or model evaluation are all strong options for salespeople who want to use their judgment and communication skills without a phone-heavy role.
