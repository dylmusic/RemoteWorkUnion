Handshake AI Application Under Review: What It Means and What to Do

What Handshake AI application under review actually means, how long to wait at each stage, and what to do instead of refreshing your dashboard.

If your Handshake AI application says under review, the most useful thing to understand is that it usually means your information has been submitted and is being considered, but no final decision has been made. Under review is not rejection. It is also not a guarantee of placement. For remote AI training platforms, application review is often tied to project availability, skill matching, and timing.

The waiting period is where most applicants lose momentum. They refresh their dashboard, wonder if something went wrong, and do nothing productive while one platform moves slowly. This guide explains what under review means for Handshake AI applications, what to check, how long to wait at each stage, and what to do instead of sitting still.

What "Under Review" Means for Handshake AI Applications

Under review for the Handshake AI Fellowship program and Handshake AI projects means your application has passed the submission stage and is being evaluated. Handshake AI's own help documentation says applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and that project invitations depend on project needs, qualifications, experience, area of expertise, partner demand, and project availability.

That means the review process is not always a straight line from application to yes or no. Your profile may be sitting in a queue while the platform waits for a project that matches your background. A reviewer may have seen your application and noted it as a potential fit for a future project opening. Or your application may still be waiting for initial review if the platform is processing a high volume of submissions.

Under review is different from no decision has been made. It typically means the process is active. But active does not mean fast, and it does not mean automatic. The outcome depends on factors outside your control — but there are steps that keep you in the best possible position.

Common Reasons an Application Stays Under Review

Several factors can explain why a Handshake AI application stays in under review status for an extended period without a resolution.

High applicant volume. Remote AI work attracts a large number of applicants. Platforms with rolling review processes may take longer when their queue is full. This is not a reflection of your qualifications — it is a logistics issue on the platform's side.

Project demand is uneven. Handshake AI work is project-based. The platform may need ten legal researchers this week and twenty medical writers next month. If there is no active project that fits your profile right now, your application may sit in review status until one opens up.

Your background may not match any currently active project. Even a qualified applicant may not move forward if the specific skills or domain knowledge the platform needs right now do not match what you have listed. This is why profile specificity matters more than a general list of abilities.

Location, language, or authorization requirements. Some Handshake AI projects are location-specific, require fluency in a particular language, or have work authorization requirements. If your profile does not clearly address these, the review process may stall.

Profile is too general to route. If your profile says general remote worker or does not clearly identify what kind of AI work you can evaluate, the platform cannot easily route you to a project. A vague profile can stay in review indefinitely because there is no clear project bucket to assign it to.

A Practical Waiting Timeline

There is no official published timeline for Handshake AI application review. The rolling basis review process means different applicants may wait very different amounts of time. A practical framework helps you know what to do at each stage rather than waiting passively.

Days 1 to 3: Confirm the application is complete. Check your email inbox, spam folder, and dashboard for any missing steps, required assessments, or incomplete fields. Many review delays are caused by something the applicant missed in the submission process.

Days 3 to 7: Review your profile for obvious gaps or mismatches. Is your domain expertise clearly stated? Does your resume include keywords that match AI evaluation work? Is your availability accurate? Do not contact support unless there is an actual technical issue. A polished profile is a better use of this time.

Days 7 to 14: Apply to other relevant AI training platforms. Mercor, Outlier AI, micro1, and other evaluator platforms can all run in parallel with a Handshake AI application. Do not build your plan around one outcome when you have no confirmed timeline.

After 30 days: Treat the application as uncertain. Keep the account and profile active and updated, but shift the majority of your energy to new applications and platforms. Remote AI work is project-based across the entire market, not just Handshake AI. A month without a response usually means there is no current project match — not that you have been permanently disqualified.

What to Check Before Assuming You Are Stuck

Before concluding that your application is delayed for reasons outside your control, go through a checklist of things you can verify on your end.

  • Every required application checklist item is complete and submitted
  • Your resume uploaded correctly and is in a readable format
  • You completed any required assessments or screening steps that appeared in your dashboard
  • Your profile clearly reflects expertise relevant to AI evaluation work
  • You have checked your spam folder and promotions tab for any missed messages from Handshake AI
  • Your contact information is accurate and you can receive email at the address on file
  • Your account is in good standing and you can log in without issues

Many applicants who believe they are stuck in review are actually waiting on a step they have not completed. A missing assessment or an incomplete profile section can stall an application without any notification.

What to Do While You Wait

The waiting period is an asset if you use it well. Here are the highest-value actions to take while your Handshake AI application is under review.

Improve your profile with AI training keywords. Add accurate terms that describe your evaluation ability: AI model evaluation, response ranking, rubric-based review, data annotation, fact-checking, domain expertise in your field. Do not stuff keywords randomly — put them where they describe real skills you can demonstrate.

Make your expertise specific. Not just "writer" but "technical writer with experience evaluating AI-generated explanations for accuracy and instruction following." Not just "researcher" but "academic researcher with experience in literature review, source verification, and structured annotation." Specificity helps the platform route your profile to the right project.

Apply to other platforms in parallel. Mercor, Outlier AI, micro1, LinkedIn AI evaluator postings, and other platforms can all receive applications at the same time. A strong applicant does not wait for one platform — they build a pipeline.

Practice evaluator skills. Use the waiting period to develop the core skill that AI training platforms pay for: comparing two AI-generated answers and explaining which is better and why. Practice this with free AI tools. Compare two responses for accuracy, completeness, clarity, instruction-following, tone, and safety. Write a three-sentence explanation. Do it consistently until it feels routine.

Keep a simple application tracker. Track every platform you apply to with columns for date applied, profile status, assessment status, follow-up date, and notes. This prevents confusion when managing multiple applications and helps you identify what is working.

Should You Follow Up?

Contact support only if there is a real technical issue. If an assessment link is broken, if an upload failed, if your dashboard shows an error, or if your account appears to be inaccessible — those are legitimate reasons to contact Handshake AI support.

For normal review delays, a single polite follow-up after two weeks is reasonable if there is a clear support or contact channel available. Keep the message short: confirm the application was received, mention your strongest relevant qualification, and ask if there are any additional steps you should complete. That is enough.

Do not send repeated messages. If the platform says applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, repeated follow-ups will not speed up the matching process. They may signal impatience to a recruiter who has not yet matched your profile to an available project. Your time and energy are better spent on profile improvement and new applications.

What Makes You More Likely to Advance

The applications most likely to convert from under review to a project invitation are specific. Your profile should communicate exactly what kind of AI work you can evaluate. Writing, legal reasoning, finance, coding, math, education, healthcare, research — pick the lane that matches your real background and make it the center of your profile.

Generic "remote worker" profiles are harder to match because there is no obvious project bucket to assign them to. A platform matching applicants to expert review projects needs to know: can this person evaluate a finance AI explanation? A legal brief? A healthcare summary? A coding answer? A math problem solution? The clearer your answer to that question, the faster you can be routed.

Beyond domain specificity, the strongest profiles also show evaluation skills explicitly. Not just "I have experience in X field" but "I have reviewed X-type content for accuracy, reasoning quality, and clarity." That framing matches the actual task description for most AI training work and makes the platform's job easier.

The applications that advance fastest are not the most impressive on paper. They are the ones that make it easiest to understand where the applicant fits in under thirty seconds.

Availability and reliability signals also matter. If your profile clearly shows when you are available, how many hours per week you can commit, and that you have completed other remote contract work, it reduces the perceived risk of inviting you to a project.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Handshake AI application under review mean?

Under review means your application has been submitted and is being evaluated on a rolling basis. It does not mean you have been accepted or rejected. Project invitations depend on project needs, qualifications, expertise match, and partner demand.

How long does Handshake AI take to review applications?

Handshake AI reviews applications on a rolling basis with no fixed response timeline. Timing depends on project availability, expertise match, and current demand. Give the process one to two weeks before shifting energy to other platforms.

Should I contact Handshake AI support if my application is under review?

Contact support only for technical issues like a broken assessment or failed upload. For normal review delays, improving your profile specificity and applying to other relevant AI training platforms is more productive than waiting.

What should I do while my Handshake AI application is under review?

Confirm every required application step is complete. Improve your profile to clearly show your expertise for AI evaluation work. Apply to other platforms like Mercor, Outlier AI, and micro1. Practice evaluator skills so you are ready when a project invitation arrives.

Final Takeaway

Under review is not the end of your search. Confirm your application is complete, give the process a reasonable window, and keep applying. Remote AI work is project-based. The strongest applicants treat the search as a pipeline, not a single application waiting for a decision.

Improve your profile specificity during the waiting period. Make it easy for a project matcher to route you to the right work. Apply to Mercor, Outlier AI, micro1, and other platforms so you are not dependent on one timeline. Practice evaluator skills so that when a project invitation does arrive — from Handshake AI or anywhere else — you are ready to move forward.
